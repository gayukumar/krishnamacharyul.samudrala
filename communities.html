Your family's tradition, followed faithfully

Every community does things its own way. Before each ceremony, he goes over your family's customs, gotra, sutra and family deity so the rites are performed as your elders would expect.

Sri Vaishnava

Iyengar (Thengalai)

Ceremonies in the Thengalai sampradaya of Sri Ramanuja and Manavala Mamunigal, with the Thengalai namam, Vedic mantras and Divya Prabandham pasurams recited as each family's custom asks.

Often requested: Weddings, Upanayanam, Seemantham, Tiruvaradhanam, Shraddham

Tamil Smartha (Dravida)

Iyer

Rites for Iyer families performed by their own sutra and shakha, from Nichayathartham through Kashi Yatra, Oonjal and Maangalya Dharanam at the wedding.

Often requested: Weddings, Upanayanam, Aavani Avittam, Seemantham, Shashtiabdapoorthi, Tarpanam

Andhra Pradesh & Telangana

Telugu

Telugu ceremonies with their own customs, such as Jeelakarra Bellam and Talambralu at the wedding, and the Satyanarayana Vratam kept by so many Telugu homes.

Often requested: Pelli (wedding), Gruhapravesam, Satyanarayana Vratam, Upanayanam, Ugadi

Tamil

Tamil family ceremonies and festivals, from Valaikappu for the mother-to-be to weddings and house-warmings done the Tamil way.

Often requested: Weddings, Valaikappu & Seemantham, Grihapravesam, Puthandu, Pongal

Kannada ceremonies with their own customs, including Kashi Yatre and Dhare at the wedding and the household festivals of Karnataka.

Gujarati weddings with Ganesh Sthapana, Mandap Muhurat, Hasta Milap and the Mangal Pheras, and the pujas Gujarati families keep through the year.

Vedic, havan-centred

Arya Samaj

Rituals in the Arya Samaj way set down by Swami Dayananda Saraswati: centred on the havan and Vedic mantras, without murti puja, following the Sanskar Vidhi.
