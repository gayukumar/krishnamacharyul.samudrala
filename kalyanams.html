Kalyanams, the celestial weddings of the deities

Performed with the full wedding rites for the Lord and His consort, at your home, a temple or for a community gathering. Sponsoring a kalyanam is a cherished offering for a family's blessings, a marriage, or a vow fulfilled.

Hosting a kalyanam

A kalyanam can be held at your home, at a temple, or for a community or association gathering. Many families sponsor one on a festival day, for a marriage in the family, or to fulfil a vow.

Once the date and place are settled, he will go over everything the ceremony needs, from the utsava murtis or images of the Lord and His consort to flowers, garlands and puja items, so the family can prepare with confidence.

To plan a kalyanam, share

  • Which kalyanam you would like
  • The date or festival you have in mind
  • Where it will be held, and roughly how many will attend
