Hindu priest in Southern California

Vedic rituals for Southern California families, since 1983

For more than four decades, Krishnamacharyulu Samudrala has performed pujas, homas and life ceremonies for Hindu families in Malibu, Los Angeles, San Diego, Riverside and the Inland Empire. Many households have now called him across three generations, from grandparents' weddings to grandchildren's naming ceremonies.

By the grace of Lord Venkateswara

Every ceremony begins with a prayer to the Lord of the Seven Hills, for the well-being of the family and all who gather.

A family priest for three generations

Over four decades he has become a family priest in the traditional sense: the person a household calls for every important moment, and then calls again when the next generation reaches the same moments.
