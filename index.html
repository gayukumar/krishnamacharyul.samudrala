Ceremonies

Every ritual a Hindu family needs

Ceremonies are performed at your home, a temple or a hall, following the Vedic procedure for each rite. If you don't see the ceremony you need, ask. It can almost certainly be arranged.

Life samskaras in the order they come in a life

  1. BirthNamakaranaNaming ceremony for a newborn
  2. InfancyAnnaprashanaBaby's first solid food
  3. ChildhoodChaula / MundanFirst haircut
  4. LearningAksharabhyasaBeginning of education
  5. YouthUpanayanaSacred thread ceremony
  6. MarriageVivahaWedding, with engagement and pre-wedding rites
  7. EldersShashtiabdapoorthi60th birthday, and later milestone birthdays
  8. RemembranceAntyeshti & ShraddhaLast rites and annual remembrance

About

A family priest for three generations

Krishnamacharyulu Samudrala has served Hindu families across Malibu, Los Angeles, San Diego, Riverside and the Inland Empire since 1983. Over 43 years he has become a family priest in the traditional sense: the person a household calls for every important moment, and then calls again when the next generation reaches the same moments.

Each ceremony is performed with care for the Vedic procedure and with time taken to explain what each step means, so that children and guests can follow and take part.

1

Grandparents

Weddings, house-warmings and pujas when families first settled in Southern California.

2

Their children

Naming ceremonies, sacred thread ceremonies and weddings for the next generation.

3

Grandchildren

Today, the same families call for their own children's first ceremonies.
