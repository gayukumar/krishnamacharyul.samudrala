Horoscope reading in the Vedic tradition

Jyotisham, Vedic astrology, is one of the six Vedangas and is called the eye of the Veda: it shows when a rite should be performed and what the time ahead holds. Alongside the ceremonies, he reads and matches horoscopes and fixes muhurthams the traditional way, from the panchangam and your exact birth details.

ज्योतिषम्

Why a horoscope reading matters

  • A map of the life aheadYour horoscope (jatakam) is a picture of the sky at the exact moment and place of your birth. In the Vedic tradition it is read as a guide to your nature, your strengths and the periods of life to come.
  • The right time for every beginningHindu ceremonies are begun at an auspicious muhurtham. Jyotisham is how that time is found, so that a wedding, a house-warming or a new venture starts with the blessings of the grahas.
  • Harmony in marriageFor generations, families have compared horoscopes before a marriage. Matching looks at temperament, health, family life and children, and gives both families confidence in the alliance.
  • Preparation, not fearA good reading does not frighten. When a difficult period or dosha appears, it explains what it means and the prayers and remedies that bring peace of mind, so the family can face it calmly.

For a reading, share

  • Date of birth
  • Exact time of birth, as close to the minute as you can
  • Place of birth (town and country)
  • For matching: the same details for both the bride and the groom
