Life samskarasIn the order they come in a life. Choose one to request it.

  1. BirthNamakaranaNaming ceremony for a newborn
  2. InfancyAnnaprashanaBaby's first solid food
  3. ChildhoodChaula / MundanFirst haircut
  4. LearningAksharabhyasaBeginning of education
  5. YouthUpanayanaSacred thread ceremony
  6. MarriageVivahaWedding, with engagement and pre-wedding rites
  7. EldersShashtiabdapoorthi60th birthday, and later milestone birthdays
  8. RemembranceAntyeshti & ShraddhaLast rites and annual remembrance

Home & new beginningsBlessings for a new home, land, vehicle or venture.

Griha PraveshRequestHouse-warming when moving into a new home Bhoomi PujaRequestBlessing the land before construction begins Vastu ShantiRequestBringing harmony to a home or workplace Vahana PujaRequestBlessing a new car or vehicle Business openingRequestPuja for a new shop, office or venture
