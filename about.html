Since 1983

Krishnamacharyulu Samudrala has served Hindu families across Malibu, Los Angeles, San Diego, Riverside and the Inland Empire since 1983. Over four decades he has become a family priest in the traditional sense: the person a household calls for every important moment, and then calls again when the next generation reaches the same moments.

Each ceremony is performed with care for the Vedic procedure and with time taken to explain what each step means, so that children and guests can follow and take part.

1

Grandparents

Weddings, house-warmings and pujas when families first settled in Southern California.

2

Their children

Naming ceremonies, sacred thread ceremonies and weddings for the next generation.

3

Grandchildren

Today, the same families call for their own children's first ceremonies.

ॐ नमो वेङ्कटेशाय

By the grace of Lord Venkateswara

Every ceremony begins with a prayer to the Lord of the Seven Hills, for the well-being of the family and all who gather.

How a ceremony is arranged

  1. Get in touchCall, or send a request with the ceremony and the date you have in mind.
  2. Choose the dateSettle the date and time together, including an auspicious muhurtham if you want one.
  3. PrepareGo over what the ceremony needs, from puja items to the space where it will be held.
  4. The ceremonyAt your home, a temple or a hall, with each step explained as it happens.
